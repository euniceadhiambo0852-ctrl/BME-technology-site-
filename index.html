01 / Who we are

Big ideas deserve to be built, tested and made useful.

BME stands for Beyond Machine Evolution. Our ambition is to create technology that addresses real problems and can grow from useful first products into a broader technology ecosystem.

We are starting in Kenya with curiosity, disciplined experimentation and a commitment to build responsibly. Our global ambition will be earned through products that work and people who trust them.
